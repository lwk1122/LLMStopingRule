license. When merging into an existing repository, review and retain its license
rather than replacing it automatically.

license is included at `editing/vendor/LICENSE.txt`; the source version and
commit are recorded in `SOURCE_MAP.md`. Retain those files and the upstream
notices when redistributing the matching component.
